Privacy policy

truenas backup is used only by its owner, to store encrypted backups of their own NAS in their own Google Drive.

  • It can only see and change the files it created itself in that Drive (Google's drive.file access).
  • It collects no personal data, has no other users, and sends nothing to anyone besides the owner's own Google Drive.
  • Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.
